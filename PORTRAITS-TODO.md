# Portraits still to do

Round 9 checked all 31 requested people on 23 November 2026 and added 15 drawings. The labels name **169 people: 145 portraits and 24 empty places**. Of the 24, **16 remain pending** after this search and **eight stay empty by instruction**. See [all 31 outcomes](docs/portraits/round 9/REVIEW.md), [source records](docs/portraits/round 9/PHOTOGRAPHS.md) and [checks](docs/portraits/round 9/CHECKS.json).

Ten new drawings follow reusable pictures. Five are studies from two independent named photographs; their publication rights remain uncertain. Seven exports are 84×105 because their sources are small or soft. Full-size PNGs are working masters, not evidence of extra source detail.
