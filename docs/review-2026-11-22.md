# Adversarial review, 22 November 2026: triage

Nine reviewers each attacked one workflow (first visit, walk, tours, piece pages, people, languages,
accessibility, offline, search arrival). Their reports, screenshots and scripts are kept outside the repository.
Below, every finding, sorted into what is warranted, what is worth improving, and what is left, with the reason.

## C. Left, with the reason

- The Spanish tours are in the voice of a guide from the River Plate (voseo), while the labels use the Spanish of
  Spain. Deliberate, by the owner's decision of 22 November 2026: the guide need not be from Spain. Button names in
  her text stay as the screen shows them.
- Esperanto letters with a circumflex or breve: Sofia Sans has no such glyphs, so the browser draws them from
  another face. Left until a typeface with them is chosen.

- One lastmod date for all pages: true and harmless; search engines treat it as a hint.
- The English address redirecting by browser language: deliberate, and it can be undone from the menu.
- zh-TW downloading two files once: small, rare.
- Still pictures with old clock times: they are photographs of a moment, and the model runs on a tap.
- Ways in more than a screen down on phones: the board is the welcome; a short line with the three ways in
  could go above it, left for the design pass.
- Typing /walk/: the walk lives at #walk; a folder would duplicate every walk page. A redirect page is cheap and
  could be added with B4.
- Kept recordings copied into memory on every page open: measured, and to be fixed with B12.
- Guide starting before the visitor arrives: seen only with the software renderer.
