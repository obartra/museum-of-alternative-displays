# Sounds of the machines: what to download

The museum plays a machine's own sound, or none. A recording goes in only if its page says it is the real machine
and its licence allows a public website to use it with credit (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Nothing synthesised, nothing
that only sounds similar. Searched on 22 November 2026, on Freesound, Wikimedia Commons, the Internet Archive and
BigSoundBank. Every licence below was read on the recording's own page.

Freesound needs a free account to download. Save each file under the name in the last column and hand them over
together. The museum trims them, converts them and prints the credit on the piece's page.

## Left out on purpose

- Film and slide projectors for the Eidophor. They are real projectors, and they are not an Eidophor.
- A room humidifier for the fog screen. A stand-in.

## Nothing found under an open licence

Flip-disc display (two rounds of searching), mechanical television, colour wheel television, vector display,
kinetic mirror, shape display, braille display, persistence-of-vision fan, swept-volume display, laser plasma,
scanned laser, micromirror colour wheel, Nixie tube, plasma panel. The storage tube was not searched. These stay
silent until a real recording turns up, or until someone who owns the machine records it.
