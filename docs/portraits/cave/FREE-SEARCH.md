# Free-picture search

## Carolina Cruz-Neira

Started with [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Cruz-Neira) and [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Carolina_Cruz-Neira).

- [Dr. Cruz-Neira.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dr._Cruz-Neira.jpg): Commons states own work by tcoffin, CC BY-SA 4.0, dated 10 January 2020. Its description names her at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, passing the identity gate. The native file is 1832×1253 and listed as 235 KB. The original image endpoint returned 403. Downloaded UCF copies show the same earlier scarf/server-rack scene; their exact sizes and measured face widths are c04/c05. The 2024/2025 reuse dates do not make it a new photograph. Not selected for the requested latest appearance.
- [VPRO interview frame](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carolina_Cruz_on_how_virtual_reality_will_shape_the_world_of_tomorrow.png): CC BY-SA 4.0, author VPRO, dated 6 July 2016. Commons’ cropped file is 258×258 and 105628 bytes; the actual downloaded Open Images source frame is 512×288 and 209653 bytes (c06). The direct photo-page institutional/CAVE gate is incomplete. It is also older and soft, and is not a model input.

A recent UCF study was chosen to meet the requested apparent age. The existence of the older licensed photographs is not concealed or described as “no free picture found.”

## Daniel J. Sandin

Checked Wikipedia and Commons searches. [MyronKrueger DanSandin 1999.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MyronKrueger_DanSandin_1999.jpg) is by Dave Pape, dated 11 September 1999, CC BY 2.5. It is a 297×246 two-person scene, listed as 32 KB. The direct description does not identify the sitter with the CAVE/lab or specify the side. The original image endpoint returned 403, so no face width is invented. It would also give a much earlier appearance. Not downloaded into the reference set and not used.

The EVL 50th-anniversary paper, [2024, CC BY 4.0](https://www.evl.uic.edu/documents/evl_50th.pdf), includes a 1975 group photograph without a sitter key suitable for this task. A general article licence and a list of authors do not identify every face in a group. It was not used as a portrait source. The named 2024 Video Data Bank interview supplies a sharp, recent identifiable face; it has no open licence.

## Thomas A. DeFanti

[Commons’ category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Thomas_A._DeFanti) contains a scientific report title page rather than a usable portrait. The 2024 EVL paper does not provide an adequately keyed recent likeness. No usable recent Commons portrait was obtained.

The [UCSC author page](https://ucsc-ospo.github.io/author/thomas-a.-defanti/) says in its footer that the work is licensed CC BY 4.0. A full-size original portrait is available and names the correct researcher with CAVE/EVL context. No separate photograph credit or rights statement was found; the scope of the general footer for that headshot remains uncertain. Its earlier-looking appearance supports structure, not the age target. The selected study follows the thinner white hair in the [2026 HPCwire feature](https://www.hpcwire.com/35-hpc-legends-tom-defanti/), whose photograph has no stated open licence.

## Access limits

Older www2.evl.uic.edu profiles were readable through search/browser extraction, while direct downloads repeatedly failed TLS. Their newer www.evl.uic.edu counterparts do not expose the same legacy entry.php image paths. The 2025 Fresno State speaker page names DeFanti, but does not itself supply the requested CAVE/laboratory link and direct download failed; it was not used as a photograph input. These are access or gate results, not proof that an image never existed.
