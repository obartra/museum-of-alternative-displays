# Portrait style, CAVE supplement

The approved reference is Mary Lou Jepsen 2.3, matched to Gabor, Leith and Upatnieks. It is a pen drawing for the museum’s quiet catalogue and white-walled walk.

- Vertical 4:5 head and upper shoulders; complete documented crown, modest empty margin. No frame, text, hands, scenery or props.
- Warm ivory background, target #f7f6f0; near-black ink, target #14171a. Fine economical contours, approximately 2–3 pixels at a 1024-pixel master width, finer inside. Vary weight: lighter across the top, firmer on the shadow side. No uniform heavy outline.
- Hair and clothing carry tone with restrained hatching and documented dark masses. Bare forehead, cheeks, nose, chin and neck stay open ivory. No stipple, hatch shadows, invented wrinkles or photographic gradients. Essential eyelids, lips, nostrils, brows and documented age folds remain fine contours. Real moustaches, beards and stubble remain.
- Preserve source-supported head proportions, apparent age, hairline, glasses and facial hair. A publication or upload date is not automatically a photograph date. Younger secondary photographs support structure, not age.
- After drawings retain the source pose and clothing and require a recorded reuse basis. Studies use named published photographs, two where usable independent views exist, and a new pose and clothing. Redrawing does not clear rights.
- Do not invent source detail or use an unconfirmed sitter. Judge whether visible detail supports a likeness rather than applying a rigid pixel cutoff. A documented crop can remain cropped (Marie Brown); a second named view may establish a clipped hair boundary (Benton). A clearly named historical engraving is acceptable even if made after death (Frisius); keep dates in provenance without adding special public portrait notes.
- Small/soft photographs produce only 84×105 exports; ordinary exports are 600×750 WebP. Neck and cheek hatch shadows are removed while actual facial hair and essential contours remain.

All drawings were generated/edited with the built-in image_gen tool. Selected prompt chains and hashes are in prompts.json. PNG masters and private research references remain outside this portable package. WebP export only resizes/crops the generated artwork; it does not retouch the face.

This supplement uses ordinary 600×750 exports because multiple named views provide usable structural detail. The small/soft rule still applies to a source used alone; no source is upscaled to pretend it contains new detail.
