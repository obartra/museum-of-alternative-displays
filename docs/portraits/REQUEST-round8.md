# Portrait request, round 8

Written on 22 November 2026. Nine people on the labels have no portrait yet and are real, known people whose
photograph may exist. The other eight empty places stay empty on purpose: three have no documented likeness
(Mozi, Ibn al-Haytham, Ibn Daniyal), four are not identified well enough to look for (John Myer, A. Frankenstein,
W. von Jaworski, Auguste Berthier), and John Ralston is left empty by the owner's decision.

Rules as in rounds 6 and the CAVE supplement (`docs/portraits/cave/STYLE.md`, `REQUEST.md`): free pictures first,
otherwise a study from two named photographs; the open-skin Jepsen 2.3 pen style; 600×750 WebP, or 84×105 where the
only source is small or soft; every photograph recorded with its page, naming words, publisher, photographer, year,
licence as found, bytes and face width. **The identity gate is the main rule:** a photograph counts only if the page
it is on names the person together with a fact from the museum's label, below. A namesake is not the person.
