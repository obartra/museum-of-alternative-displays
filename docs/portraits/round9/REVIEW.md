Source softness remains a material limit, especially for Dawe, Seibel, Moore and the newspaper halftones. No large export is supplied for those seven.

## Rights and date limits

Five studies—Julius Popp, John West, Eric J. Seibel, Greg Dawe and Brian T. Schowengerdt—use reference-only photographs with **uncertain publication rights**. No permission or release was obtained; drawings do not clear rights. Their source photographs are absent from the public site and repository. Each has two genuinely different photographs; duplicate directory copies were rejected.

Lotte Reiniger and Furness follow CC BY-SA 4.0 photographs; their adapted drawings retain that attribution/licence. Other after-pictures follow the recorded Commons public-domain basis. Edwin Land uses only the Gotfryd/Library of Congress picture with its recorded reusable basis. Commons metadata, source citations and VRT statements are retained without inventing a stronger grant.

Reiniger follows 1970; Furness follows the free 2014 photo. Seibel’s age/hairline follows the captioned 2019 group. Dawe’s gray hair follows the captioned June 2017 view, not the earlier author portrait. Popp uses two 2017 event views. Schowengerdt’s later appearance is published on a 2020 page, but capture year is unknown. West’s current headshot is undated; the sharper magazine view was published 2015 with a 2014 filename clue. Kircher’s inscription 1655/Commons 1665 and Hammond’s caption 1923/Commons 1932 discrepancies are explicitly retained.
