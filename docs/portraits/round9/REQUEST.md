# Portrait request, round 9

Written on 23 November 2026. 31 people on the labels have no portrait yet and are real, known people whose
picture may exist: the ten from round 8, still open, and 21 added with the newest pieces. Eight places stay empty
on purpose: Mozi, Ibn al-Haytham and Ibn Daniyal (no documented likeness), John Myer, A. Frankenstein,
W. von Jaworski and Auguste Berthier (not identified well enough), and John Ralston (the owner's decision).

Rules as in round 6 and the CAVE supplement (`docs/portraits/cave/STYLE.md`): free pictures first, otherwise a
study from two named photographs; the open-skin Jepsen 2.3 pen style; 600×750 WebP, or 84×105 where the only
source is small or soft; every picture recorded with its page, naming words, publisher, photographer, year,
licence as found, bytes and face width. Clearly named historical engravings and paintings are accepted where the
detail supports a likeness. **The identity gate is the main rule:** a picture counts only if the page it is on
names the person together with a fact from the gate below. A namesake is not the person.
