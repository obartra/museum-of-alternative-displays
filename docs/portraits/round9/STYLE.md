# Portrait style / round 9

Use approved Jepsen 2.3, matched beside Gabor, Leith and Upatnieks. Vertical4:5 head and upper shoulders; complete source-supported crown with modest blank margin. No text, frame, hands, props or scenery.

Near-black economical pen on warm ivory (targets #14171a and #f7f6f0). Fine varied contours: lighter above, firmer on the shadow side. No uniformly heavy border. Hair and clothes carry restrained directional hatching and dark masses. Bare scalp, forehead, cheeks, nose, chin and neck stay open ivory. Essential feature contours and documented age folds remain; no stipple, hatch shadows, photographic skin gradients or invented wrinkles. Actual facial hair remains.

Retain source-supported proportions, apparent age, hairline, spectacles and facial hair. Publication, upload and camera dates are distinguished. A younger secondary view supports structure, not age. Historical paintings/engravings must be clearly named and detailed enough; posthumous date alone is not a rejection.

An after-picture follows a source with a recorded reuse basis. A study in this round requires two distinct named photographs and changes pose and clothing. Two crops of one exposure do not count. Neither a new pose nor redrawing establishes publication rights. Uncertain-rights photographs and source comparisons remain private; the public site receives drawings and source-page credits.

Identity gate: the page publishing the image must name the person with the requested invention/employer/context. No face recognition or namesake substitution. In a group, caption position must identify the sitter.

Ordinary WebP 600×750; small/soft sources 84×105 with small:true, no large public export or wall portrait. A large generated PNG cannot upgrade weak evidence. Use native source crops without enhancement; keep actual file bytes, raster dimensions and approximate manual face bounds.

All creative generation and cleanup used the built-in image_gen tool. Pillow only made reference crops, review sheets and final resized WebPs. Exact prompts and source/master hashes are in prompts.json.
