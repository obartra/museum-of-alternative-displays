# Museum portrait specification

The approved Jepsen 2.3 drawing fixes the set’s line weight. Preserve all existing 38 assets byte for byte, including that version. A style reference controls marks and layout, never the new sitter’s face.

Use a 4:5 crop, complete documented crown and upper shoulders, near-black ink #14171a on pale enamel #f7f6f0. At 1024 px width, use a varied silhouette about 2–3 px, heavier on the shadow side and lighter across the top. Use finer eye, nose and mouth contours. Allow sparse contour-following hatching in hair and clothes, with solid black for deepest shadows. Keep forehead, cheeks, chin and neck open: no stipple, decorative hatching, invented wrinkles or skin marks. Brows remain essential facial features. No photo gradients, frame, text or signature. AI output is visually inspected; exact flat RGB colour is not guaranteed by prompting.

For a drawing after a cleared photo, preserve pose, gaze, apparent age, visible clothing, hairline, eyewear and expression. Do not reconstruct an undocumented crown or invent teeth. A small source is not made more informative by enlarging the output. Native cheek width below 60 px stops drawing; 60–119 px limits a draft to a few marks and label-size review. Readability can require a stricter hold even above those thresholds. No face restoration or generative sharpening.

The latest supplied brief governs publication: a verified likeness and a documented open licence, public-domain basis or applicable permission are required. A study or redrawing is not a rights clearance. Where a Commons grant conflicts with provenance, retain the uncertainty and keep the drawing outside the site lookup.

Keep the source page, author/credit, actual grant, capture-year basis, native dimensions, crop and comparison. Distinguish a photograph’s creator from the archive, uploader or courtesy contact. Do not transfer an article’s CC licence to an excluded third-party photo. Do not infer public-domain status from a government-hosted copy or the absence of a copyright notice.
