# Identifying the remaining 15 people

A record of someone’s work can establish the contributor without supplying a face. These links distinguish a biography/profile from an identifying publication or patent record. “Not obtained” does not mean no likeness exists.

## John Myer

Split-flap board. [Cifra 5 design credits, ADI Design Museum](https://www.adidesignmuseum.org/en/collection/cifra-5-electro-mechanical-clock/) (record).

Identity unresolved: split-flap sources describe a Belgian inventor. A portrait of MIT architect John Randolph Myer was not accepted without confirming they are the same person.

## Kenyon Taylor

Flip-disc display. [Kenyon Taylor, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyon_Taylor) (biography).

Searched the engineer’s full name Maurice Kenyon Taylor and nickname; IEEE DATAR history and Computer History Museum oral histories confirm the person. Ferranti-Packard archive autobiographical notes dated15February1970 are a concrete lead, not an obtained portrait. No captioned adequate face accepted.

## Oleg Tretiakoff

Refreshable braille display. [Tretiakoff and Tretiakoff, US patent 4,044,350](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4044350A/en) (record).

Recovered C.A.Technology’s archived company and P2RD pages (2007–2008); they describe assistive-reading devices and contain no usable named portrait. Patent records establish inventor Oleg B.Tretiakoff; the old cadet image remains unconnected to him.

## Andrée Tretiakoff

Refreshable braille display. [Tretiakoff and Tretiakoff, US patent 4,044,350](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4044350A/en) (record).

Expanded to accented/unaccented forms, maiden surname Asseo from patent4,305,067, company archives and computational-linguistics author records. Goodreads namesake page contains only a default placeholder. No named adequate portrait accepted; no biography inferred from genealogical namesakes.

## Auguste Berthier
